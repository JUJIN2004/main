Spell with the ASL alphabet — show your hand to the camera

Recognizes ASL letters (A–Z) as words. Spell a word with your hand; when you pause (lower your hand for about a second), the app speaks the word and starts the next. Use one hand, palm toward the camera. Clear view and good lighting help. J and Z need motion and may not be detected.
